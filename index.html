Ideja

Katër gjethe, një për secilën pjesë të shtëpisë

A four-leaf clover is luck, and a gift from Clover should feel lucky. Each leaf is one thing the house does, and each takes that part's colour. By day the house serves coffee and matcha; after 17:00 it turns into a wine bar, and the whole palette turns with it. Try the Ditë / Mbrëmje switch at the top.

LuleFlowers and bouquets. Peony.
KafeCoffee and matcha. Matcha green.
ËmbëlsiraSweets and small treats. Honey.
VerëWine and meze, the evening. Wine.
