Logo

Katër gjethe, një shtëpi.

A four-leaf clover is luck, and a gift from Clover should feel lucky. Each leaf is one thing the house does: lule (flowers), kafe and matcha, ëmbëlsira (sweets) and verë (wine). Each leaf takes that part's colour, and the stem holds them together. The wordmark is Gloock, lowercase.

Anatomia

One leaf for each part of the house

LuleTop right, leaf-lule. Flowers and bouquets, the reason the house exists.
Kafe · matchaBottom right, leaf-kafe. The day: coffee, matcha, the counter.
VerëBottom left, leaf-vere. The evening: wine and meze. Lighter in the evening palette so it holds on the dark ground.
ËmbëlsiraTop left, leaf-embelsira. Sweets and the small treats, honey-coloured.
KërcelliThe stem drops through the cut between the lower leaves and never touches them. Its colour is ink.

Hapësira

Clear space and minimum size

The unit x is the height of one leaf. Keep at least 1x clear on every side of any lockup; nothing else enters that zone, edges of the page included.
