# Applications

The brand on real objects: the fascia, a takeaway cup, a bouquet tag, a business card, the day and evening menus, and two Instagram posts.

- Day objects (cup, tag, day menu, day posts) are built in the Ditë palette; evening objects (wine menu, event posts) in the Mbrëmje palette. Wrap an object in `data-theme="light"` or `data-theme="dark"` to pin its palette regardless of the reader's theme.
- The fascia is `wine` with the evening lockup, all day.
- Names and contact details on the business card are placeholders.
