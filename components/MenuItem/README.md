# MenuItem

A row on the menu: the name, one line of what is in it, the price in DM Mono.

- Names in Albanian as guests say them: “Makiato e madhe”, “Matcha latte”, “Pjatë meze për dy”. Notes are ingredients, not adjectives: “qumësht tërshëre, mjaltë”.
- Prices are `€3.50` style: euro sign first, two decimals. A glass and a bottle go as “€4 / €22”.
- Stack rows directly; the hairline (`line`) separates them. Group them under a `display-md` section title.
