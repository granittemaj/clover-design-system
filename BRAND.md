# Clover brand book

**Clover · coffee · wine · flowers.** A small, sunlit place: coffee in the morning, wine in the evening, flowers all day. A four-leaf clover is rare, so it brings luck. Three leaves are what the house does well (kafe, verë, lule); the fourth is luck, and it comes in with the guest. The look is golden-hour light on unbleached paper and kraft, drawn by hand in espresso ink.

## Voice

- Warm, short, a little playful. Write like the person behind the counter talks.
- Albanian first. English appears where guests expect it: the descriptor "coffee · wine · flowers", menus (as a second line) and signage for visitors.
- Lowercase for the descriptor and small labels; sentence case everywhere else. The wordmark is always "Clover" with a capital C.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji. A small hand-drawn doodle (a heart, a daisy) is fine on menus and boards.
- Prices: euro, two decimals ("2.50"), "nga 20.00" for from-prices.

Lines to reuse:

> Kafe në mëngjes, verë në mbrëmje, lule gjithë ditën.
> Tre gjëra që i bëjmë mirë. E katërta është fati.
> Shihemi te Clover.

## Logo

The mark is a four-leaf clover drawn as one continuous pen line per leaf, with a stem that curves gently out from the centre. The pen never quite closes each leaf at its base, so the centre stays open and light. The wordmark is "Clover" in Allison, a light signature script, outlined; the descriptor sits underneath in Mali Light, lowercase and widely spaced. Files are in `assets/Logos/`.

- **Primary:** `clover-lockup-stacked-en-ink.svg` (clover above "Clover", descriptor below). Use it on cups, bottles, tags, boxes, menus and the shopfront.
- **Horizontal:** `clover-lockup-horizontal-*` for narrow spaces: receipts, website header, email.
- **Round sign:** `clover-badge-*` for the hanging sign, stickers and box seals. Cream, espresso and kraft versions.
- **Mark alone:** `clover-mark-*` where the name is already nearby, and for embossing and stamps.
- **Albanian descriptor:** every lockup also comes as `-sq-` with "kafe · verë · lule".
- **Colour:** espresso on cream, paper or kraft; cream on espresso or wine; wine on blush or cream. Never on busy photos without a paper panel.
- **Clear space:** one leaf's height on every side. **Minimum size:** full lockup 30 mm wide, mark 8 mm.
- Don't redraw the clover, add a fifth leaf, fill the leaves, change the line weight, or retype "Clover": use the files so the signature stays exact.

## Type

- **Allison:** the wordmark's signature hand. Use it only for short words: the name, labels, menu section titles, two or three words at most. Never for sentences, never smaller than 32px.
- **Mali:** soft and rounded. Headlines in Light, body text, menu descriptions, prices, and the descriptor (lowercase, letter-spaced).
- Both cover the full Albanian alphabet, including Ç and Ë.

## Materials and imagery

- Unbleached paper cups with an espresso lid; cream bottle labels; kraft boxes and bouquet wrap tied with twine; galvanised buckets of flowers at the door; a round cream sign on a black bracket.
- Photography: warm afternoon sun, leaf shadows on walls and tables, flowers loose and seasonal (daisies, blush roses, eucalyptus). No filters that turn the cream cold.

## The room

Cream stone or lime plaster walls, black-framed glass doors, warm pendant lights, open shelves with bottles, a marble counter, rattan chairs outside, flowers at the entrance. The leaf shadows on the website are the light this room should have at four in the afternoon.
